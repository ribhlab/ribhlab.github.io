Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This privacy policy applies specifically to NovaGrid, the Android puzzle game with package identifier com.novagrid.cosmicpuzzle. It explains how game data is stored locally and how third-party advertising and consent services may process data.

1. Information stored on your device

NovaGrid uses local application storage to remember scores, best scores, completed levels, in-game points or stars, language preferences, sound/music settings and game progress. You do not need to create a NovaGrid account to play. The game's core puzzle engine does not send these locally saved progress records to a NovaGrid-operated account or game server.

2. Ads and third-party services

NovaGrid integrates Google's Mobile Ads (AdMob) software development kit and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising-related consent choices. Banner ads are disabled. The app may show interstitial ads at natural breaks in gameplay. Depending on SDK configuration, region, user choices and internet access, Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Device and app identifiers (which may include the Android advertising identifier, when available).
  • IP address and approximate geographic area inferred from the IP address.
  • Ad impressions, interactions and basic app usage signals relating to ads.
  • Technical diagnostics, device information and consent preferences.

This processing can be used to deliver, measure and protect advertising, prevent fraud and, when permitted, personalize ads. Google and its partners operate under their own policies. Read How Google uses information for advertising and Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Optional Google Play purchase — remove ads

NovaGrid may offer an optional one-time non-consumable in-app purchase through Google Play Billing to remove interstitial ads from the game for the purchasing Google Play account. Google processes the payment; the NovaGrid app does not receive or store your payment-card number. NovaGrid queries Google Play for the purchase state and purchase token to activate or restore the ad-free entitlement, including on a new device. Refunds or cancelled purchases can revoke the entitlement. Google's Privacy Policy applies to its billing service and related account, device and transaction information.

4. Internet access and consent

The core puzzle game can be played offline, but ad and consent services may need an internet connection. Where required, the app presents Google consent choices or advertising privacy options. You can continue playing if an ad is unavailable or consent is not granted.

5. Children's privacy

NovaGrid's age targeting is declared separately in Google Play Console. Advertising configuration and consent obligations must follow the applicable rules for the selected audience. We do not knowingly ask players to provide their names, home addresses or account passwords as part of the core game.

6. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on the device until you clear NovaGrid's storage or uninstall the app. You can erase local progress in Android Settings → Apps → NovaGrid → Storage → Clear storage. This removes saved local data and cannot be undone. Third-party advertising data is governed by the relevant third party's own retention and deletion practices, as described in its privacy policy.

7. Your privacy choices

Where Google requires an in-app advertising privacy control, you can use the game's Ad choices option. Your device's privacy and advertising identifier settings may provide additional controls. For data processed by Google, consult Google My Ad Center and the Google privacy links above.

8. Security and changes

We aim to minimize app-operated collection of personal data. No networked service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. This policy may change as NovaGrid features, SDKs or legal requirements evolve. The date above indicates its latest revision.

9. Contact

For questions about privacy or data requests, contact the NovaGrid developer at rachid022note4@gmail.com. You can also find this email under developer contact details in the Google Play listing. Do not share passwords or sensitive personal information.

This policy applies to the NovaGrid Android game, not to the broader website hosting this page. The game itself may offer local offline play while Google advertising services can process data as described above.

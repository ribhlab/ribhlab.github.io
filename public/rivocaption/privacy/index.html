Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy applies to RivoCaption: Video Captions for Android (package com.rivocaption.editor). RivoCaption helps users import videos, transcribe speech, edit subtitles, apply caption styles, translate text, and export captioned videos.

1. Video, audio, and captions

When you choose a video or audio recording, RivoCaption uses the selected content to perform editing, subtitle generation, local transcription, caption styling, and video export. These operations ordinarily take place on your device. Imported video, audio, caption projects, and exports may remain in app storage or folders you choose. RivoCaption does not require you to create a developer account for the core local editor.

2. Language models and optional cloud transcription

On-device speech recognition may download language models and related resources from their provider when you request them. The application also supports an optional cloud transcription route, but it is not enabled by default; if cloud transcription becomes available, transmitting your audio or other content to a remote service must be a separate user-initiated action with clear consent. The applicable cloud service will process transmitted content according to its terms and privacy policy. In the initial release, the app's cloud endpoint is not configured and cloud processing should not be represented as active.

3. Advertising, analytics, and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). If advertisements are displayed, Google and its partners may collect or process advertising IDs, device identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, ad impressions and interactions, crash diagnostics and consent settings, for showing ads, measuring performance, security, and fraud prevention. Applicable consent choices are displayed where required. See Google advertising technologies and Google's privacy policy.

4. Permissions and sharing

Access to videos, media, microphone or device storage is requested only when relevant to the editing or recording action you choose, subject to Android permissions. Exporting or sharing a captioned video with another application occurs only when you initiate it. Once shared, the destination service's privacy practices apply.

5. Retention and deletion

Projects, selected local media and generated captions may remain on your device until you delete them or clear the application's data. You can delete projects in-app where supported, uninstall RivoCaption, or use Android Settings → Apps → RivoCaption → Storage → Clear storage to remove app-private local data. Exported files stored elsewhere or shared with other apps may not be removed by clearing this app's data. Third-party advertising providers control retention of their service records.

6. Security and children

RivoCaption seeks to keep editing tasks local by default, but no software can guarantee perfect security. The app is an editing tool and is not designed for children under 13. Age targeting and any required advertising restrictions are declared in Google Play Console.

7. Updates and contact

We may revise this policy as app features, model sources, advertisements or regulations evolve. For privacy questions or data deletion inquiries, contact rachid022note4@gmail.com.

How the Google Play test will work

  1. Use an Android phone/tablet with the Google Play Store and a Google account. Volunteers must be 18 or older.
  2. Email us voluntarily from the Google account you plan to use for testing, plus your device and Android version. Never send a password.
  3. Once a signed Play test build has been released, we'll send eligible volunteers the Google Play opt-in link. The app is not yet available for enrollment.
  4. Open the invitation, opt in with the same Google account, install from Google Play, and provide honest feedback on a few features.
  5. If you opt in for our closed test, please remain opted in for at least 14 continuous days and test during that period. You may stop participating whenever you choose.

Sending an email does not automatically enroll you in Google Play. Joining the test is optional and requires your own opt-in; Google Play controls eligibility and availability.

Privacy and feedback

Use sample documents; don't send scans of passports, IDs, financial or medical records, or other confidential files to us. A brief description of what happened, your device type, and steps to reproduce are enough. We use your volunteered contact information only to coordinate the test and reply to feedback. To request removal from the volunteer contact list, email the same support address. Google may process your account information under its own terms when you enroll in Play testing.

See the RivoScan privacy policy for information about app features, optional AI and advertising.

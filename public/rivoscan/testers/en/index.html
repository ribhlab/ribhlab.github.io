How the Google Play test will work

  1. Use an Android phone or tablet with Google Play and a Google account. Volunteers must be at least 18.
  2. Email us voluntarily using the Google account you plan to test with, plus your device and Android version. Never send a password.
  3. Once a signed Google Play test build is ready, eligible volunteers will receive an opt-in link. Enrollment is not available yet.
  4. Open the invitation, opt in with the same Google account, install from Google Play, and share honest feedback.
  5. If you join the closed test, please remain opted in for at least 14 consecutive days and try the app during that time. You may leave at any time.

Sending an email does not automatically enroll you in Google Play. Participation is optional and requires your explicit opt-in; Google controls test eligibility and availability.

Privacy and feedback

Use sample documents only. Do not send passport, identity, financial, medical, or confidential scans. A brief issue description, device model and steps to reproduce are enough. We use voluntarily supplied contact details to coordinate testing and answer feedback. Email us to request removal from the volunteer contact list. Google may process account information under its terms when you enroll.

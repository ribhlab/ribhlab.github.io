RivoScan — Data Deletion Instructions

RivoScan is an Android PDF and document scanning app. Most document files and app preferences are stored locally on your device. The app does not require a RivoScan user account for its core scanning and PDF functions.

Delete app-local files and preferences

  1. Before deleting anything, export or back up any documents you want to keep.
  2. On Android open Settings → Apps → RivoScan → Storage → Clear storage. The exact menu wording varies by phone.
  3. Alternatively, uninstall RivoScan. This removes the app and normally its app-managed local data. Files that you exported or saved outside the app may remain and must be deleted separately using your device's file manager.

Warning: Clearing storage can permanently erase locally saved scans, recent activity and settings. Exported files, backups or content shared to other apps are not automatically deleted by this action.

Request help with privacy or deletion

For a question about personal data handled by RivoScan or a request for deletion of information that may be held by its developer, email rachid022note4@gmail.com with subject RivoScan data deletion request. Describe which information you are asking about. Do not send passwords, API keys or private document contents by email.

RivoScan uses external services for advertising and optional AI functions; those providers may independently process and retain information according to their own policies. Clearing RivoScan storage does not delete data held by Google, advertising vendors or third-party AI providers. Refer to the respective provider's privacy and deletion controls if you used those services.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy applies to the RivoScan Android app (com.ribhlab.rivoscan) developed by RibhLab. It describes document processing, optional network features, and the third-party technologies included in the current Android project. It does not describe the separate NovaGrid game or the RibhLab editorial website.

1. Camera, files and on-device tools

RivoScan can use your device camera to capture documents and photos. You select the files you want to scan, process or share. Core functions such as page ordering, PDF merging, cropping, conversion, annotations and signatures are designed to run on your device. The core PDF toolkit does not require a RivoScan account or automatically upload your documents to an RibhLab-operated document server.

Temporary preview and export files may be written to app-specific local storage or cache. Files you explicitly save or export to another location remain subject to that location's storage and deletion settings.

2. Optical character recognition and language models

RivoScan uses OCR and document scanning technologies, including Tesseract and Google ML Kit features. OCR or translation language models and scanner components may be downloaded from Google or third-party infrastructure when first needed. These downloads may expose ordinary network information, such as IP address and device or application details, to the service provider. Core OCR and offline translation are designed to process the selected content locally after model components are available.

3. Optional cloud AI — user-initiated

RivoScan includes optional AI document question-answering and problem-solving features. These use a key provided by you for the supported AI provider. The app requests an explicit action and confirmation before sending selected text, an image or a question to that provider for processing. Only use this feature when you are authorized to disclose the selected content. The third-party provider processes any data you send under its own privacy and retention policies; review OpenAI's Privacy Policy for the currently configured endpoint. Do not submit passwords, bank details, sensitive IDs or confidential records unless you understand the consequences of sharing them.

The RivoScan project labels the user-entered AI key as session-only. Third-party AI services and the provider may log or retain transmitted content according to their terms. No AI processing is necessary for basic local PDF editing and scanning.

4. Advertising and consent

The current Android project integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and a consent interface, using Google's demonstration ad identifiers during testing. Depending on actual build configuration, region and choices, ads or ad-related service requests may occur. Google and its partners may process device and app identifiers (including an advertising identifier where available), IP-derived approximate location, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and consent preferences for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. See Google advertising technologies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where available and applicable, advertising consent and privacy controls are offered in the app. Ad availability may affect optional ad-unlocked tools. Do not assume the document scanner requires agreeing to personalized advertising.

5. Optional direct device-to-device file transfer

RivoScan includes a WebRTC-based file transfer feature that requires you to exchange connection codes with the other participant. Google's public STUN service may be contacted to help the devices establish connectivity and can observe ordinary connection metadata such as public IP addresses. Transfer content is intended to be sent directly between the chosen devices; network configuration may prevent the feature from working.

6. Local preferences, retention and deletion

App preferences, UI language and some recent operation metadata may be stored locally. To delete app-local preferences and cached data, use Android Settings → Apps → RivoScan → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app. Exported files elsewhere on your device are not necessarily deleted by uninstalling. Google, third-party model providers and AI providers control their own server-side retention and deletion practices.

7. Security and permissions

RivoScan requests access to the camera or selected files when you use the corresponding feature, subject to Android permission controls. Access is needed only for the relevant action. You can revoke permissions using Android Settings. The final release's precise permission and SDK behavior should be checked against the signed Android App Bundle before submission.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the app changes its network processing, advertising setup, cloud services or data-handling practices. The date above reflects the most recent policy revision. Policy information does not replace the formal Google Play Data safety declaration, which must match the final distributed binary and all third-party SDK behavior.

9. Contact

For RivoScan privacy or support questions, please contact the RibhLab developer at rachid022note4@gmail.com.

Important: Cloud AI and peer-to-peer transfers are optional. Selecting an external sharing or cloud action may disclose information to the recipient or provider you chose. Basic PDF tools remain available without uploading documents to a RibhLab-operated server.

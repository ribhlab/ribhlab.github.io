Request deletion of RivoNFC data

Published by RibhLab · Updated 27 November 2026

Send a data-deletion request

RivoNFC has no account system. To request deletion of any information that RibhLab may hold in relation to RivoNFC, send an email from an address at which we can respond. Write RivoNFC data deletion request in the subject, describe which data you want deleted, and avoid sending NFC passwords, tag contents or other secrets. We will respond to the request and explain what we can delete or what must be handled by another provider.

Contact: rachid022note4@gmail.com.

Data stored on your Android device

RivoNFC reads and writes NFC tags locally and may keep scan history, templates, virtual-card text, reminders and app settings in private device storage. We do not operate an online database of your NFC scans, tag UIDs or encryption passwords. You can erase the app-managed private device data without submitting a request:

  1. Open Android Settings → Apps → RivoNFC → Storage & cache.
  2. Choose Clear storage / Clear data, or uninstall RivoNFC. Labels can differ by phone.
  3. Be aware that clearing app data permanently removes local templates and scan history; back up only information you want to keep.

Content previously written to physical NFC tags, QR codes, shared messages and exported files is outside app-private storage. Those copies are not automatically deleted by clearing the app. Delete them from their respective storage locations or compatible NFC tags yourself.

Advertising and third-party information

Starting with version 0.6.4, Google Mobile Ads and the User Messaging Platform may process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, ad interactions, app/device diagnostics and consent preferences. RibhLab does not receive NFC tag contents through the advertising SDK. These providers may retain information under their own policies. For controls concerning your Google account, ads and identifiers, see Google My Ad Center, Google Privacy Policy and Android settings for deleting or resetting your advertising ID. We can help direct a request to the relevant provider but cannot promise to erase records held independently by Google or other parties.

What is deleted and what may remain

On receiving a deletion request, we will review any applicable information under RibhLab's control and describe the action taken. RivoNFC does not require a registered account; app-private NFC history and templates are removed when you clear its app storage. Information you published or exported to other apps, physical NFC tags, backups you control, or data independently held by third-party advertising partners may remain and must be handled at their source. Some records may need to be retained where legally required.

Demande de suppression – Français

RivoNFC ne nécessite aucun compte. Pour demander la suppression d'informations éventuellement détenues par RibhLab, écrivez à rachid022note4@gmail.com avec l'objet « RivoNFC – suppression des données ». Pour effacer l'historique et les modèles locaux, allez dans Android → Paramètres → Applications → RivoNFC → Stockage → Effacer les données, ou désinstallez l'application. Les données écrites sur des tags NFC, fichiers exportés ou reçues par des tiers ne sont pas automatiquement effacées. Les informations publicitaires de Google suivent ses propres contrôles de confidentialité.

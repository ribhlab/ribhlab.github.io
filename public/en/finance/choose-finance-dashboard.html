Start with your workflow

A reporting application will not fix inconsistent source files by itself. Before comparing software, write down who updates the data, how often it changes, what the reconciled source of truth is, and which reports matter most. A solo business owner and a five-person finance department can have different needs even when both look at sales, margins, invoice aging and cash flow.

Offline HTML: for local individual reviews

The Offline edition suits a professional who can export supported CSVs and prefers to review data in a desktop browser without running a server. Its strengths are a simpler installation model, visual management KPIs, budget comparisons, aging, rule-based data quality alerts and JSON backups. It is not the best choice when many colleagues need simultaneous access to one centrally managed database.

Self-hosted Team: for controlled shared access

The Team edition has a Python server with SQLite storage, individual accounts and Admin/Manager/Analyst/Viewer roles. The owner must host the application, secure access and manage backups. This can be a better model for staff coordination, but it also needs technical maintenance. There is no managed SaaS subscription included, and it does not eliminate the need for accounting reconciliations.

Two-in-one bundle: a choice, not a merged product

The bundle contains both editions as separate downloads. That may be useful if an owner needs offline personal review and the same organization separately maintains a team workspace. The editions do not automatically synchronize. Evaluate whether owning two separate products will reduce friction or simply introduce another dataset to maintain.

The five checks to perform before purchasing

First, read the software and operating-system requirements. Second, verify whether an offline browser page or your own hosted Python service is suitable. Third, inspect actual listing screenshots, not only marketing mockups. Fourth, understand what must be imported manually. Fifth, confirm buyer-file contents, digital-download policy and license on Etsy before payment.

An example decision

Imagine a manager preparing monthly reporting with one CSV export from an ERP. A local dashboard could be enough after reconciliations. Now imagine three staff who must review the same centralized figures with distinct access rights: a hosted Team instance may be appropriate. If both needs exist independently, consider the bundle. Neither application becomes a regulated accounting system or a guaranteed forecast tool.

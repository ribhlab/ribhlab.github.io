Free CSV import readiness checklist for finance dashboards

Before building charts, prove that the underlying numbers are trustworthy. This independent workflow checklist works with many reporting tools—not only our ProfitLens software.

Download fictional sample CSV ↘

1. Confirm your reporting scope

  • Write down the source system, export date and reporting cut-off.
  • Specify whether the figures include VAT/sales tax and whether values are signed or absolute.
  • Define how credit notes, returns, cancellations and intercompany transactions are represented.
  • Confirm the currency and whether exchange rates were applied consistently.

2. Test data integrity

  • Check each document's unique identifier and flag duplicate IDs within the same source.
  • Ensure every transaction has a valid date, an amount, a currency and an appropriate category.
  • Inspect negative amounts: a refund or credit note should not silently inflate sales.
  • Check decimal separators, encoding, thousands separators and ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD).
  • Keep personally identifying information out of demos and publicly shared files.

3. Reconcile before importing

For each category and month, total the exported amounts and compare them with an approved source report or general-ledger control account. Record unexplained differences before publishing any dashboard. A chart based on unreconciled records may be visually convincing and financially wrong.

Example: If source sales equal 100,000 but imported sales equal 101,500, inspect duplicates, sign conventions, tax treatment and date filters. Never conceal the 1,500 difference with an arbitrary balancing entry.

5. Validate the first dashboard

  • Start with fictional sample data in a test environment.
  • Verify imported record counts, totals, monthly buckets and date filters.
  • Check receivables aging against due dates, not just invoice dates.
  • Export or back up your work and confirm that recovery works.
  • Document who approved the import and the final reconciliation.

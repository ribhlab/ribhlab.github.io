Free month-end reporting control: verify the numbers before importing

A dashboard can highlight trends, but it cannot fix inconsistent source reports. Before importing CSV files into any finance tool, run this small reconciliation using a fictional example:

  1. Sales completeness: compare the invoice register with the general ledger revenue total for the same period, currency and tax basis. Investigate cancellations and credit notes rather than forcing a match.
  2. Receivables: start with opening customer balances, add invoices and subtract payments and credits. In a fictional example, 12,000 + 8,000 − 5,000 − 1,000 = 14,000 closing receivables. Compare that with the customer subledger and control account.
  3. Cash: compare recorded receipts and payments with the bank statement, separating outstanding items and fees. Do not treat a bank balance as accounting profit.
  4. Import hygiene: use consistent date formats, unique document IDs, explicit signs for credits and no real client data in trial imports. Keep the original exports and a dated backup.

Decision rule: if the control totals do not reconcile, investigate upstream before using the dashboard for management decisions. This checklist is free educational material from the software seller, not accounting advice or a claim that ProfitLens performs automatic ledger reconciliation.

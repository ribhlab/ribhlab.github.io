RivoTalk Privacy Policy

Effective 26 November 2026 · Android package com.ribhlab.rivotalk

This policy describes the privacy practices of RivoTalk, a nearby Bluetooth voice and messaging application provided by RibhLab (Mor Inc on Google Play).

1. Local communication and permissions

RivoTalk uses Bluetooth and the microphone to establish local sessions and transmit push-to-talk audio and text to participating nearby devices. It requires nearby-device permissions and microphone access to perform these functions. Audio and messages are carried through encrypted local links; the session host relays communications and can access audio, including private conversations routed through it. This is not end-to-end encryption against the host. Session messages are held in memory; the app is not designed to record calls. Other participants may hear, receive or independently retain information you share. Only connect to people you trust and verify the displayed security code.

2. Local data and identity

The app uses a locally stored cryptographic device identity and may retain settings and blocked-device information to support authentication and session management. It does not require a RivoTalk account, phone number or payment. Local communication does not require internet access or a SIM card. Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove app-managed local data. This cannot remove information already received or saved by other participants.

3. Advertising and consent

Versions that include Google AdMob may use internet access to request banner or interstitial ads. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or share IP addresses (which may be used to estimate general location), device and advertising identifiers, product interactions, advertising data, performance information and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. These services are separate from local Bluetooth communication. RivoTalk does not intentionally send session audio or chat content to advertising services. Ad requests are subject to applicable consent choices and availability. Where available, use the in-app advertising privacy options and Android advertising settings to manage choices.

Google's practices and controls: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services · My Ad Center.

4. Retention, deletion and requests

Local session data is used for communication and app operation; locally persisted identity and settings remain until cleared or uninstalled. Information processed by Google advertising services is subject to Google's retention policies and controls. If you contact the developer, your contact details and message may be used to respond to your request and retained as needed for support or legal obligations. You may request access or deletion of information held by the developer by email.

5. Security and children

RivoTalk is intended for general nearby communication and is not designed as a service for children or as an emergency communications system. No communication system can guarantee complete security. Keep your device updated, review who can join your session and avoid sharing sensitive information with untrusted participants.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the app or its third-party services change. Contact RibhLab about RivoTalk privacy, support or data requests at rachid022note4@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords or confidential conversation contents.

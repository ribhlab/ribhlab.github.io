RivoMeasure — Measure & Plan

RivoMeasure by RibhLab brings practical measuring tools to Android: manually entered room dimensions and 2D/3D floor plans, GPS area and distance tools, a calibrated screen ruler, a spirit level, reference-based camera measurements, saved projects and compatible-device ARCore room scanning.

Availability: Version 1.3.9 has been submitted for Google Play review; it is not yet confirmed publicly available. The app contains advertising. Measurements are estimates, not certified surveying or safety-critical results.

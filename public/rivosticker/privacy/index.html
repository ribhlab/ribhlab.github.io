Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy applies to the RivoSticker Android app (com.ribhlab.rivosticker) published by RibhLab. It explains how your photos, saved stickers, advertising information, and optional sharing actions are handled.

1. Photos and local editing

When you choose a photo through Android's photo picker or other system selection features, RivoSticker processes the selected image to create stickers and photo designs. Core editing features, including cropping, captions, erasing backgrounds and saving projects, are designed to operate on your device. Images and stickers may be kept in the app's private local storage or in locations you select for export. The developer does not operate an image-upload server for these core features and does not require a RivoSticker account.

2. Image-processing components

RivoSticker includes on-device image processing technologies, including Google ML Kit and MediaPipe components. Some components may need to download model resources when first used, which can involve ordinary network and device information being processed by their provider. The images selected for local editing are not intentionally uploaded to a RibhLab-hosted photo-processing service.

3. Advertising and consent

Production versions of RivoSticker integrate Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Depending on app settings, consent, region, network access and ad availability, the app may display banners and other ad formats. Google and advertising partners may process advertising identifiers (where available), IP-derived location, device and app information, ad impressions/interactions, diagnostics and consent preferences for ad delivery, measurement, protection and fraud prevention. Some advertising can be personalized where permitted and consented to. Read Google's advertising technologies and Google's privacy policy.

Where applicable, the app requests advertising consent and may present privacy options. Android's advertising privacy controls and Google My Ad Center offer additional choices. Advertising service data may be retained by Google and partners according to their own policies.

4. Sharing and WhatsApp sticker packs

RivoSticker sends a sticker or image to another app only when you choose a share or pack-install action. The selected destination app (such as WhatsApp) handles the shared content under its own privacy policy. Sharing is optional; no message is sent automatically on your behalf.

5. Local storage, retention and deletion

Saved local projects and app-specific sticker files generally remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall RivoSticker. You can also use Android Settings → Apps → RivoSticker → Storage → Clear storage to erase private app data. Any files exported to other folders or shared with other apps may remain there and are not necessarily removed by uninstalling RivoSticker. Google and other services control deletion and retention of their own service records.

6. Permissions and security

Access to device photos or the camera, where offered, is used for actions you initiate. Android's permission settings let you revoke relevant access. RivoSticker aims to minimize app-operated transmission of user-created content, but no connected service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Children and audience

RivoSticker's target-age selection is declared in Google Play Console. If the application is made available to younger audiences, applicable family, advertising and privacy requirements must be followed. The editor does not require users to create an account or provide a name, home address, or password to create stickers.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy to reflect new app functionality, SDK versions, privacy controls or legal requirements, and update the date shown above. Questions about privacy or deletion can be sent to RibhLab at rachid022note4@gmail.com.

This policy concerns RivoSticker only. The Google Play Data safety disclosure should be read together with this policy and must reflect the final distributed build and applicable third-party SDK behavior.

TERMINAL GUIDE

Read the chart. Don't chase the chart.

Choose a crypto pair and timeframe. The SMC engine works on confirmed swing pivots, labeling structure breaks (BOS), changes of character (CHoCH), three-candle Fair Value Gaps, order block candidates and liquidity sweeps.

Draw: pick Horizontal, Trend or Fib from the chart toolbar, then click one or two chart points. Click the cursor tool to stop drawing. Use the indicators menu for EMA, volume, RSI and overlays.

Prices: historical candles come from public Binance Spot data; updates are delivered via public WebSocket where available. A blocked feed is reported as an error—there are no simulated live prices.

Important: patterns are historical heuristics and are not verified institutional order flow, investment advice, or a guarantee of profit.

# Privacy & Families safety – Tiny Turbo Racers v0.1

This first development build runs **offline**. It does not include AdMob, analytics, trackers, account registration, external network calls, third-party SDKs, social sharing, or purchases.

Car selection, selected world, colors and star score are stored on the device in WebView localStorage only. There are no dangerous Android permissions in the manifest.

The game is designed for young children. Before introducing monetization, review the current Google Play Families requirements, use only an approved Families Self-Certified Ads SDK, disable personalized advertising, verify the ad creative and format, implement the correct privacy disclosures, and test child-safety flows. Adding AdMob is **not** complete in this build.

This document describes the current code, not a promise of future compliance approval by Google.
